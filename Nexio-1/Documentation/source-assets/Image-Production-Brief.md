# NEXIO Image Production Brief — Editorial Intelligence

## Art direction
Warm editorial, restrained, architectural, human-centred. Use generous whitespace, refined serif display typography, quiet teal accents, tactile paper-like surfaces and natural-light classroom imagery.

## Required photorealistic image batch (next stage)
1. Wide classroom hero, 16:9, clean composition with clear panel focal point.
2. Realistic interactive flat-panel studio product render, front three-quarter view, no readable product specs or invented branding.
3. Modern Indian school classroom, daylight, restrained materials, genuine architectural photography.
4. Close-up detail crop of the panel glass, bezel and touch interaction.
5. Classroom ecosystem still life: OPS module, wireless sharing receiver, audio, mounting system and accessories.
6. Technology support / installation environment, no visible fake company identity.

## Guardrails
- No AI robots, glowing brains, circuit-board backgrounds or generic sci-fi imagery.
- No fake customer logos, school names, testimonials, awards or numerical claims.
- Do not place illegible text into product screens; prefer clean abstract lesson visuals.
- Do not present illustrative product renders as verified commercial product photographs.
- Preserve realistic classroom proportions, screen mounting and lighting.
