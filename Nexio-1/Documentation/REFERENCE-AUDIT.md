## Insights applied to this concept

### Upstream
- Editorial feel through restraint: one dominant headline, generous whitespace and refined type. This shaped the white-forward hero with a serif headline.
- Subtle interaction polish rather than effects. This shaped the hairline underlines and 3–4px lifts.
- **Do not copy:** its brand-advisory wording and its client identity.

### Carefront
- Serif headlines with warm paper tones read as trustworthy without looking clinical. Used here as Instrument Serif on white, with paper kept for alternate sections only.
- Reusable Nav, Footer and CTA components keep long pages coherent. Mirrored in the shared header, footer and Cta.
- **Do not copy:** the healthcare-SaaS positioning, sign-up and onboarding flows, and pricing toggles.

### Talentflow
- Product pages alternate explanation and imagery. This became the alternating feature storytelling on the Interactive Flat Panels page.
- A consistent header and footer system across many pages.
- **Do not copy:** HR/SaaS product demos, pricing tables and illustrated software UI.

### Fynora
- Service presentation that builds trust (clear service pages, a confident hierarchy). This became the five-pillar editorial index.
- Conversion sections placed at page ends. This became the closing CTA on every page.
- **Do not copy:** financial-advisory content, pricing packages and the testimonial-led trust devices (NEXIO has no approved testimonials).

### Structa
- Service architecture for multi-discipline technical firms. This informed the Products & Services page structure (pillars, then catalogue).
- Semantic heading hierarchy as a credibility signal.
- **Do not copy:** case studies, team and careers sections, and the blog. These are out of scope, and there is no verified content for them.

### Zephiq
- Calm palette and generous spacing for technical services.
- Restrained motion only. GSAP-style reveals were adapted using Motion.
- **Do not copy:** AI-agency messaging, 'hours saved' outcome stats and glass dashboard cards.

### Nexa Growth
- Light theme with concise section transitions.
- A footer wordmark as a closing moment. Here this became the serif tagline in the footer.
- **Do not copy:** stat cards with results figures (none are verified for NEXIO), the pastel palette and the review slider.

### Velox
- Numbered sections (01–06) with small metadata labels. This became the mono 'Fig. 01' captions and section indices.
- Photography-led sequencing with captions under images.
- **Do not copy:** the studio showreel framing, the custom 'view' cursor (the brief rules out cursor-follow) and drag-to-scrub galleries.

### LUMIÈRE Premium
- Minimal hero copy over clean space, with a sober section order: hero, story, services, CTA.
- Restrained editorial typography pairing.
- **Do not copy:** wedding/portrait positioning, 'starting price' service lists and the testimonial.

### Taped
- Reduced-motion-aware reveal interactions.
- Global type and spacing variables. This became the CSS custom-property token system.
- **Do not copy:** its whiteboard / sticky-note portfolio metaphor and playful tone. NEXIO is not a designer portfolio.

### Althor
- Every section moves toward one action. Each page ends in a single enquiry CTA.
- Design system defined once and reused.
- **Do not copy:** AI-agency language, the 'book a call' framing and case-study results claims.
