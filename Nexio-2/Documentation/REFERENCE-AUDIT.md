## Insights applied to this concept

### Upstream
- Hierarchy and whitespace even in a technical system.
- **Do not copy:** its brand-advisory wording and its client identity.

### Carefront
- Interactive module overview on the home page. This inspired the modular classroom mini-grid.
- Reusable CTA component.
- **Do not copy:** the healthcare-SaaS positioning, sign-up and onboarding flows, and pricing toggles.

### Talentflow
- Feature-explanation rhythm. This became the accessible Feature Explorer tabs on the Interactive Flat Panels page.
- Layout variety across a long page: modules, strip, stage, index.
- **Do not copy:** HR/SaaS product demos, pricing tables and illustrated software UI.

### Fynora
- Clear service presentation.
- Consultation-oriented CTA placement.
- **Do not copy:** financial-advisory content, pricing packages and the testimonial-led trust devices (NEXIO has no approved testimonials).

### Structa
- IT-company service taxonomy: multi-page services for complex offerings. This shaped the coded pillar index strip and the P/01–P/05 service rows.
- Institutional trust through structure rather than decoration.
- **Do not copy:** case studies, team and careers sections, and the blog. These are out of scope, and there is no verified content for them.

### Zephiq
- Bright technology presentation with clean sans type and lime accents. This set the white/cool-grey canvas, with cobalt and a tiny lime signal.
- Restrained GSAP-style motion. This became the line-draw section heads and short reveals.
- **Do not copy:** AI-agency messaging, 'hours saved' outcome stats and glass dashboard cards.

### Nexa Growth
- Light visual system with concise section transitions, used for the 'NX/01…' section bar rhythm.
- Scroll-triggered reveals used once per section.
- **Do not copy:** stat cards with results figures (none are verified for NEXIO), the pastel palette and the review slider.

### Velox
- Metadata labels on imagery. This became the framed image modules with 'MOD 01' bars.
- Responsive gallery treatment.
- **Do not copy:** the studio showreel framing, the custom 'view' cursor (the brief rules out cursor-follow) and drag-to-scrub galleries.

### LUMIÈRE Premium
- Polished, short transitions.
- **Do not copy:** wedding/portrait positioning, 'starting price' service lists and the testimonial.

### Taped
- Tactile hover detail on cards. This became the cobalt top-edge draw on module hover.
- **Do not copy:** its whiteboard / sticky-note portfolio metaphor and playful tone. NEXIO is not a designer portfolio.

### Althor
- Single-goal conversion flow. Every module funnels to 'Talk to NEXIO'.
- Consistent design system with tokens defined once.
- **Do not copy:** AI-agency language, the 'book a call' framing and case-study results claims.
