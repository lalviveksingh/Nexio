NX/04Featured · Interactive Flat Panel

A smarter way to teach, present and collaborate.

  • Touch — Navigate, select and interact directly on the screen — intuitive for teachers and students alike.
  • Writing & annotation — Write, highlight and annotate over lessons and content as you teach.
  • Multimedia — Present images, video, documents and presentations on one large display.
  • Screen sharing — Bring content from compatible devices onto the panel, including wireless sharing options.
  • Compatible devices — Connect laptops and compatible devices so existing material can be used straight away.
  • Classroom use — Built for the everyday rhythm of teaching, presenting and collaborating in a room.
