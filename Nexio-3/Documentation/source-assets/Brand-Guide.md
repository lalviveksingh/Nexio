# NEXIO — Bold Future

**Tagline:** Make technology matter.

## Brand personality
High-contrast editorial technology brand. Use oversized confident type, strong asymmetry, warm porcelain and carbon surfaces, signal-orange details, dramatic but believable photography and expressive composition.

## Logo direction
A dynamic angular X built from two interlocking directional strokes. It should be highly legible at small sizes and feel distinctive on a display bezel, visiting card and favicon.

The supplied SVG artwork is the initial vector exploration. Before external commercial release, refine optical spacing, test minimum sizes, and confirm the final registered-name lockup.

## Typography
- Display: Sora / Space Grotesk Display
- Body: Inter
- Technical labels: IBM Plex Mono

Use the named typefaces only if their web licences permit the intended deployment. Always include robust sans-serif fallbacks.

## Motion direction
Bold but controlled word reveals, clipped image transitions, short directional movement, restrained horizontal ticker moments and precise hover feedback.

## Light and dark mode
- First visit defaults to light mode.
- Respect a saved user choice in localStorage.
- Do not auto-switch based on operating-system preference.
- Maintain equivalent contrast and visual hierarchy in both themes.

## Photography direction
Use believable education and technology environments, natural materials, true-to-life displays and realistic proportions. Avoid generic AI imagery, fabricated brand marks, fake client logos, fake testimonials, fake statistics and false product specifications.

## Current illustrative assets
The `Visuals/` directory contains custom vector concept artwork for layout exploration. These are not photographs of actual NEXIO products and do not imply verified product capabilities. Replace or supplement them with approved real product imagery before final commercial launch.
