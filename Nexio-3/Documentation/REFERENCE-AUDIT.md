## Insights applied to this concept

### Upstream
- Whitespace discipline between oversized elements.
- **Do not copy:** its brand-advisory wording and its client identity.

### Carefront
- Editorial restraint: limited palette, confident type, calm spacing between bold moments.
- **Do not copy:** the healthcare-SaaS positioning, sign-up and onboarding flows, and pricing toggles.

### Talentflow
- Alternating feature explanations.
- **Do not copy:** HR/SaaS product demos, pricing tables and illustrated software UI.

### Fynora
- Restrained professional hierarchy, which keeps the boldness readable.
- **Do not copy:** financial-advisory content, pricing packages and the testimonial-led trust devices (NEXIO has no approved testimonials).

### Structa
- IT-company page architecture for Products & Services.
- **Do not copy:** case studies, team and careers sections, and the blog. These are out of scope, and there is no verified content for them.

### Zephiq
- Clean service cards and grid logic, used for the pillar cards on carbon.
- **Do not copy:** AI-agency messaging, 'hours saved' outcome stats and glass dashboard cards.

### Nexa Growth
- Large animated wordmark as a section moment. This became the 'Innovate. Educate. Elevate.' scroll-linked band. It is not perpetual.
- **Do not copy:** stat cards with results figures (none are verified for NEXIO), the pastel palette and the review slider.

### Velox
- Image-first sequencing with numbered sections. This became the big-number stories on the panels page.
- Gallery with a sense of movement. This became a restrained scroll-linked drift on desktop and a swipe row on mobile.
- **Do not copy:** the studio showreel framing, the custom 'view' cursor (the brief rules out cursor-follow) and drag-to-scrub galleries.

### LUMIÈRE Premium
- High-end image presentation with large crops and minimal chrome.
- Clear section order ending in a strong closing CTA.
- **Do not copy:** wedding/portrait positioning, 'starting price' service lists and the testimonial.

### Taped
- Layered composition. This became the solid card overlapping the classroom photo's edge (never text on a busy image).
- Expressive hover details. This became the orange sweep on list rows and the offset orange shadow on catalogue cards.
- **Do not copy:** its whiteboard / sticky-note portfolio metaphor and playful tone. NEXIO is not a designer portfolio.

### Althor
- Strong hero hierarchy: one oversized promise, then two CTAs. This became the giant Sora hero, with 'Discover NEXIO' and 'Start an enquiry'.
- Conversion sequencing: every section ends with a clear next step.
- **Do not copy:** AI-agency language, the 'book a call' framing and case-study results claims.
